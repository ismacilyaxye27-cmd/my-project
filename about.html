Our story

We believe the best
spaces feel personal.

RINJI is a full-service painting and décor studio for homes and hospitality spaces with soul.

More than a fresh coat

Craft, curiosity
and a lot of colour.

RINJI began with a simple belief: painting should feel as exciting as the room it transforms. What started as a small team of meticulous painters is now a collaborative studio of colour lovers, finish specialists and interior stylists.

We are not interested in fast makeovers. We take time to understand light, materials, architecture and the life that will unfold within four walls.
